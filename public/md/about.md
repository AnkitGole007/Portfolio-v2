# About Ankit Gole

Ankit Gole, AI/ML Engineer, San Jose, CA.

I build LLM-based applications that help people with real workflows and daily tasks. The goal is to assist people and ease their work, not to replace whole systems.

I would rather take one application all the way to deployable, safe and secure, with observability and guardrails, than build many prototypes that never ship. Fine-tuning, evaluation and training work support that. They are not the whole job.
